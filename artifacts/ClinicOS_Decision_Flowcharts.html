A patient's visit, end to end

From walking in to walking out with a prescription. Five decisions decide the path: whether they are already on file, whether the clinic may record at all, whether this visit is recorded or typed, whether the note is ready, and whether it is shared.

Two minutes

That is what the doctor has per patient — greet, history, examine, explain, prescribe and record. The record is what gets dropped, so ClinicOS records the consultation that was already happening.

If it crashes mid-visit

Chunks are already on disk and the note is mirrored in localStorage. The unfinished visit reappears on Today with the reason it stopped.

The one-way door

Signing is irreversible: it writes the Edit diff, stamps the signature, and deletes the audio. Everything before it can be redone.

Hiding a link is not a control

One minute of sync

What the clinic computer does every minute, and how a row that changed in both places is settled. The merge rules are the same code on both sides — the only reason the two copies can be trusted to agree.

Why triggers, not queries

A clinic's clock may be wrong, so the local side counts (syncSeq) instead of trusting time, and the online side stamps with the database clock.

Receipt numbers cannot collide

The receipt_tag setting never travels, so one copy prints KTR-0042 and the other 0042 — never the same number twice.

A new table needs a decision

Does it travel at all? Left unanswered, the answer is no — and nobody notices until a clinic asks where something went.
